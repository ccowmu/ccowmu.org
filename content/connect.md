{{% panel title="Community Guidelines" %}}
Our chat is pretty simple to navigate. Follow these three rules and you'll be golden:

1. **Don't be a jerk.** Treat everyone with basic respect. No harassment, discrimination, or being unnecessarily hostile.
2. **Use common sense.** Post in appropriate channels, don't spam, and think before you type. If it seems like a bad idea, it probably is.
3. **Officers have final say.** Club officers make the final decisions on disputes, channel management, and enforcement. Their word is law.
{{% /panel %}}
