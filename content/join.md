{{% panel title="Why Join the Computer Club?" %}}
With our own office and all the resources in it, our greatest resource still remains our people. After almost 50 years at WMU, we've had hundreds of great members now off doing remarkable things at places like Google, Apple, LinkedIn, IBM, TI, Socket, and the DoD.

If you're even a little interested in security, programming, hardware, electronics, or you're just a bit geeky, then chances are you'll click with us.

The best way to see if we're the right fit is stopping by a meeting to see for yourself. Check out our [meeting minutes](/minutes/) to see what a typical meeting might entail.
{{% /panel %}}

{{< cardpanel title="How to Join" numbered="true" >}}
{{< card title="Attend a Meeting" >}}Come to any Thursday meeting at 6:00 PM in 2225 Kohrman Hall.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Pay Dues" >}}Annual membership is $10. Bring cash to any meeting, or use <a href="https://paypal.me/cclub" rel="noopener">paypal.me/cclub</a>.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Create an Account" >}}We'll help you set up chat access and add you to our systems.{{< /card >}}

{{< cardpanel title="Member Benefits" >}}
{{< card title="Workshops" >}}Hands-on learning sessions covering programming languages, dev tools, and random tech.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Projects" >}}Work on awesome projects with fellow members, from robots and talking fish to vintage computers.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Connections" >}}Network with your peers and alumni working in big tech and attend industry alumni events.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Hardware" >}}Use club equipment including servers, 3D printers, tools, and testing equipment.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Competitions" >}}Participate in game jams, hackathons, and cybersecurity competitions.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Development" >}}Resume reviews, interview preparation, and career guidance from people who've been here too long.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Snacks" >}}We stock the drink fridge and the shelves with cheap snacks. Bring a dollar or two.{{< /card >}}
{{< card title="Fast Internet" >}}The room has its own LAN with gigabit ethernet at every desk. Pull a big ISO, run a build, or host a LAN party without touching campus Wi-Fi.{{< /card >}}
